# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making this project a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive
learning community. Everyone should be able to participate, ask questions,
share ideas, and contribute without fear of harassment or discrimination.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Treat others with respect and professionalism.
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Give constructive feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Be patient with different backgrounds, skill levels, and perspectives.
- Assume good intent while addressing misunderstandings respectfully.
- Respect the privacy and personal information of others.
- Follow the project's contribution guidelines and maintainer decisions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberately hostile conduct.
- Trolling, disruptive behavior, or repeated bad-faith arguments.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or sexually explicit language or imagery.
- Publishing or sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Threats of violence or encouraging harm against any person or group.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably make someone feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull
requests, discussions, reviews, chat channels, learning sessions, and events
when someone is representing the project or its community.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the
project maintainer through the repository's private contact method. Please
include the following information when possible:

- A brief description of what happened.
- The date, time, and project location where it occurred.
- Links, screenshots, or other relevant context.
- The names of people involved, if known.

Please do not make public reports that include private or sensitive information.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers will review reports fairly and take appropriate action.
Depending on the circumstances, actions may include a private warning,
removal of content, temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project
spaces.

Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is not allowed.
Maintainers may also take action against knowingly false or malicious reports.

## Acknowledgements

This Code of Conduct is adapted from community standards commonly used by open
source projects, including the Contributor Covenant.
